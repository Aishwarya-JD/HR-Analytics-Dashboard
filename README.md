# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to provide comprehensive workforce insights, enabling HR teams and business leaders to monitor employee demographics, compensation, and leave management trends. The dashboard transforms raw HR data into actionable insights through dynamic visualizations and filtering capabilities.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- Monitor overall workforce headcount and demographics.
- Analyze salary distribution across employees.
- Track leave balances and utilization trends.
- Identify workforce patterns by gender, age group, qualification, and time period.
- Support strategic HR decision-making through data-driven insights.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### Executive KPIs
The dashboard provides a quick overview of key HR metrics:

- Total Headcount
- Total Salary Expenditure
- Minimum Salary
- Maximum Salary
- Average Salary
- Employees with Leave Balance Over 20 Days
- Total Leave Balance
- Average Leave Balance

---

### Employee Analysis

#### Headcount by Year
Displays employee growth trends from 2017 to 2023, helping stakeholders monitor workforce expansion and retention.

#### Headcount by Gender
Visualizes workforce distribution between male and female employees.

#### Salary by Gender
Compares total salary expenditure across genders to identify compensation distribution trends.

---

### Leave Management Analysis

#### Leave Balance by Age Groups
Tracks how leave balances vary among different employee age groups.

#### Leave Balance & Average Leave Balance by Year
Provides historical analysis of:
- Total leave balance
- Average leave balance

This helps HR identify leave accumulation trends and workforce utilization patterns.

---

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes multiple slicers for dynamic analysis:

#### Gender
- Female
- Male

#### Quarter
- Qtr 1
- Qtr 2
- Qtr 3
- Qtr 4

#### Qualification
- High School Diploma
- Diploma
- Bachelor's Degree
- Master's Degree

#### Age Range
Interactive slider for age-based employee analysis.

#### Year Range
Interactive slider for period-based trend analysis.

#### Job Title
Analyze metrics by specific employee roles.

#### Month
Filter insights by month.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Open using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Refresh data if required.
4. Interact with slicers and filters.
5. Explore workforce, salary, and leave-related insights.
